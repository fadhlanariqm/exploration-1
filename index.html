Copy of a copy,on purpose

Lecture notes the night before the exam. Flyers for the band's next show. A thesis that has to be bound by tomorrow. We copy it in black and white or colour, print it on the risograph, then fold, staple or bind it. Move across the page to see its negative.

Walk-ins only. Open until 2am in exam week. Bring a USB stick or the actual paper.

The idea

Every copy loses something. A little ink off the curves, a speck from the glass, a shadow where the lid would not close.

Bring us your lecture notes, your band flyer, your grandmother's recipe, and we will run it as clean as the glass allows.

Or run it seven times, until the words start to crumble, and staple that instead.

One recipe card, seven times over

This is every other copy from a run of seven. The type drops two cuts each time, and the sheet creeps further off square.

    How a job runs

    1. Step 1

      Bring it

      On a USB stick or as the actual paper. Walk in, there is no booking.

    2. Step 2

      Pick the paper

      Plain white, kraft, or a colour from the rate card, up to A3.

    3. Step 3

      Run it

      Black and white, colour, or two inks on the risograph.

    4. Step 4

      Finish it

      Folded and stapled, or bound with a hard cover, same day if it is in by noon.

    Lost the original?

    Bring any copy you have and we will run from that. It will be a little worse. Everything here is.
